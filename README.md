# Perdio-Boca
Perdió Boca is an action game developed as a prototype for the "Desafío Buenos Aires 2026" Game Jam organized by the Government of the Autonomous City of Buenos Aires.  In this game, you play as a bostero who takes action to the streets after his team loses by throwing beers at enemies and an epic final boss. Combine strategic movement, limited resource management, and quick attacks to defeat your adversaries.  🎮 My first jam - Prototype 👨‍💻 Programming, Game Design, Original Idea, and Production 🏆 Developed for Desafío Buenos Aires 2026  Controls: - Arrow Keys: Movement - X: Punch - C: Throw beer - ESC: Pause

Playable online: https://buentipojorge.itch.io/perdio-boca
